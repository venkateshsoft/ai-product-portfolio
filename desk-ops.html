What it demonstrates

DeskOps moves beyond a chart-only dashboard. Each operational area has its own data-backed view, including ticket management, SLA monitoring, major incidents, escalations, team performance, backlog aging, problem management, change coordination, governance, reports, actions, and administration.

The demo includes fictional persisted records for tickets, teams, services, escalations, incidents, problems, changes, actions, audit logs, and reporting views.
